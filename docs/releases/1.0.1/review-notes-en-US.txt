Endless Crown is a watch-only Digital Crown haptic fidget app. It has no login, backend, ads, analytics, purchases, or special permissions.

Version 1.0.1 reduces repeated timer creation and preference writes during scrolling. The existing haptic effects and per-scroll request logic are preserved.

How to test on a physical Apple Watch:
1. Open Endless Crown and rotate the Digital Crown to scroll and feel haptic feedback.
2. Tap the number line or effect chip to cycle effects, or long-press it to open the Effects picker.
3. Double-tap the number line or effect chip to open the menu.
4. Try Numbers, Statistics, Guide, Reset, Ambient Mode, and Hide Menu Icon.
5. Scroll, stop, leave the app, and reopen it to check that local statistics are retained.
6. Add the Endless Crown complication to a watch face to view the current item and haptic count.

Physical haptic feedback requires an Apple Watch; a simulator can verify the UI and model behavior.
