# Endless Crown Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

Endless Crown does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data Collection

Endless Crown does not collect data from the app.

The app stores preferences and usage statistics locally on your Apple Watch, such as the selected haptic effect, ambient mode setting, current item number, and haptic counts. This information is stored with Apple system storage APIs on your device and, where needed, in the app group shared between the watch app and its complication.

## Tracking

Endless Crown does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Accounts, Ads, and Analytics

Endless Crown does not require an account and does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or a backend service.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, email [EndlessCrown@jenny.media](mailto:EndlessCrown@jenny.media).
